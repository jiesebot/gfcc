MY STORY

Representation begins with listening.

Green Bay has given generations of people reasons to be proud of where they live. I want City Hall to reflect that pride in how it maintains our streets and parks, supports public education and workers, protects privacy, welcomes neighbors, and listens when something isn't right. Public service means showing up, doing the homework, and answering to residents.

HOW I'D APPROACH THE JOB

Principles that guide the work.

The ballot may be nonpartisan. My values are clear: dignity at work, good schools, civil liberties, and accountable government.

01 / FAIRNESS

People before labels

Listen to residents and evaluate decisions for who benefits, who bears the costs, and who might be left out.

02 / ACCESS

Everyone has a voice

Make room for people with different schedules, needs, backgrounds, and ways of participating.

03 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Clear answers

Explain decisions plainly, welcome good questions, and stay connected after Election Day.
